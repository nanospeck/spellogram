# 12. What is phototropism, and how does it occur?

Phototropism is the growth movement towards light, facilitated by the hormone auxin, which causes cells on the shaded side to elongate. Use the mnemonics below to understand its principles better.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Photo's Light Angle Shaded Cells Elongate**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic captures the essence of phototropism: it links "photo" (light) with the concept that cells on the shaded side elongate due to the action of auxin.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Pandas Highlight Others Trees' Responses, Outlining Plants' Instant Shaded-Movement Enhancement.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic emphasizes the major components of phototropism:
- "Pandas" relates to phototropism
- "Highlight" indicates the hormone auxin
- "Others" represents its effect on growth towards light
- "Trees'" denotes movement towards the light source 
- "Responses" points to the resulting growth action 
- "Outlining" connects to how it occurs on the shaded side 
- "Plants'" emphasizes that this process occurs in plants
- "Instant" reflects the prompt response to light 
- "Shaded-Movement" illustrates the side's elongation 
- "Enhancement" denotes the growth facilitated by auxin.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**PATE - Plant Aims Towards Light Elongation**

:::

**Explanation:**  
The mnemonic "PATE" specifies the key aspects of phototropism:
- **P**: Plant
- **A**: Aims
- **T**: Towards
- **L**: Light
- **E**: Elongation

It succinctly conveys that plants grow towards light via elongation caused by auxin.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Plants Angled With Lights Around, Stretch Onward Shaded.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic creates a vivid image of how plants grow:
- "Plants" indicates the subject 
- "Angled" points to the specific growth direction 
- "With Lights" signifies the light source 
- "Around" denotes the movement towards it 
- "Stretch Onward" highlights how the cells on the shaded side elongate.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Photo Likes Shady Auxins, Elongate!**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This catchy mnemonic reinforces the relationship between light and auxin: 
- "Photo" refers to phototropism
- "Likes" emphasizes the direction towards light
- "Shady" points to the side of cell elongation
- "Auxins" identifies the hormone involved
- "Elongate" captures the process itself.

---

Using these mnemonics can make it easier to remember the concept of phototropism and its mechanism of action in plants.
