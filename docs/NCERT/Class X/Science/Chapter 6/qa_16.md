# 16. How does insulin regulate blood sugar levels?

Insulin, secreted by the pancreas, helps cells absorb glucose from the blood, thereby regulating blood sugar levels. Use the mnemonics below to understand its roles.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Incredible Pancakes Help Absorb Glucose Brilliantly.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic captures the key components of insulin's function:
- **I**: Insulin
- **P**: Pancreas
- **H**: Helps
- **A**: Absorb
- **G**: Glucose
- **B**: Brilliantly (indicating blood regulation)

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Pancreas Imparts Sweet Regulation.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic reminds you that:
- **Pancreas**: Refers to the organ that releases insulin
- **Imparts**: Suggests insulin's action
- **Sweet**: Relates to sugar regulation
- **Regulation**: Refers to the overall function of maintaining blood sugar levels

---

This memorable image represents:
- **Icy**: Insulin

- **Absorb**: Clearly refers to uptake of glucose
- **Glucose**: Directly ties to the substance being regulated
