# 11. Differentiate between movement due to growth and immediate response to stimuli in plants.

Movement due to growth involves directional growth such as a seedling growing towards light, while the immediate response, like the sensitive plant's leaf folding, does not involve growth. Use the mnemonics below to aid in your understanding.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Giraffes Gallantly Dance Towards Light; Sensitive Plants Always Hide.**

:::

      **Explanation:**  
      This mnemonic helps to remember:
      - **Giraffes Gallantly Dance Towards Light**: Movement due to growth involves directional growth, like a seedling growing towards light.
      - **Sensitive Plants Always Hide**: Indicates the immediate response of plants, such as the folding of a sensitive plant's leaves.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Growing Sunflowers, Folding Mimosa**

:::

      **Explanation:**  
      This mnemonic captures key concepts:
      - **Growing Sunflowers**: Represents growth movement towards light.
      - **Folding Mimosa**: Represents the immediate response of sensitive plants that do not involve growth.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Seedling's Light Quest vs Sensitive Swift Fold.**

:::

      **Explanation:**  
      This mnemonic clarifies:
      - **Seedling's Light Quest**: Refers to a seedling's movement towards light.
      - **Sensitive Swift Fold**: Captures the immediate response of the sensitive plant's leaf folding, emphasizing that it does not involve growth.

---
