# 9. How do muscles respond to nervous stimulation?

Muscles respond to nervous stimulation by contracting, due to shape changes in special proteins within muscle cells. Use the mnemonics below to understand this response effectively.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**My Rapid Shape Change Powers Contraction**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps remember the key roles of muscle response to nervous stimulation:

- **M**: Muscles
- **R**: Respond
- **S**: Shape
- **C**: Change
- **P**: Proteins
- **C**: Contraction

---

- **Contract**: Indicates the primary response.
- **When**: Represents the condition (nervous stimulation).
