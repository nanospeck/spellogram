# 1. Explain the role of a neuron in transmitting information.

A neuron acquires information at the dendritic tip, sets off a chemical reaction creating an electrical impulse, which travels through the cell body and axon, and at the axonal end releases chemicals to continue the impulse across a synapse. Use the mnemonics below to remember the sequence of actions a neuron takes in transmitting information.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**D**etect **C**hemical **E**nergy **T**rip **A**cross **S**ynapse

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps recall the steps involved in neuronal transmission:

- **D**: Dendritic tip (where information is acquired)
- **C**: Chemical reaction
- **E**: Electrical impulse 
- **T**: Travels through the cell body and axon
- **A**: Axonal end
- **S**: Synapse (where impulses continue)

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Dendritic Dreams Electrically Across Axons Synaptically.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic illustrates the process of neuron function:

- **Dendritic**: Information starts at the dendritic tip.
- **Dreams**: Represents the chemical reaction creating impulses.
- **Electrically**: Conveys the electrical impulse.
- **Across**: Indicates traveling through.
- **Axons**: The axon pathway.
- **Synaptically**: Refers to the release of chemicals at the synapse.
