# 8. How is the brain protected physically?

The brain is protected by a bony structure known as the skull, and is cushioned in a fluid-filled balloon inside the skull. Use the mnemonics below to understand its protective features.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**B**rain's **S**afe **C**ocoon: **F**luid **B**alloon

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic highlights the main protective features of the brain:
- **B**: Brain
- **S**: Safe (embodying the protection)
- **C**: Cocoon (representing the cushioned aspect)
- **F**: Fluid
- **B**: Balloon

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Skull's Guard: Ballooned Cocoon.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic captures the protection system of the brain with:
- **Skull's Guard**: Represents the "bony structure known as the skull."
- **Ballooned Cocoon**: Represents the "cushioned fluid-filled balloon," emphasizing both protective elements.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**"Bony Skull, Fluid Pillow—Brain's Safe Fellow."**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic uses vivid imagery to connect:
- **Bony Skull**: Refers to the protective skull structure.
- **Fluid Pillow**: Captures the idea of the cushioning fluid that protects the brain.
- **Brain's Safe Fellow**: Reinforces the idea of safety for the brain within this structure.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic emphasizes:

- **The Skull Surrounds**: Aids in remembering the skull as the main protective feature.
- **A Balloon**: Represents the cushioning effect inside the skull.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**"Bony Skull Balloon Protects the Brain"**

:::

This simple mnemonic includes:
