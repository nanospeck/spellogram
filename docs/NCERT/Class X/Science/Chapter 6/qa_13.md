# 13. Give an example of how hormones affect plant growth.

Auxins help in plant growth by causing cells to elongate in response to light direction. Use the mnemonics below to remember this concept.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]
**"Aunt's tall plants lean towards light."**
:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic uses a relatable image to recall the roles of auxins:

- **Aunt's**: Auxins
- **tall plants**: Plant growth by causing cells to elongate
- **lean towards light**: In response to light direction.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]
**"Auxins Assistance: Elegant Elongation Elevates."**
:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic emphasizes the key aspects of auxins:

- **Auxins**: The hormone responsible for growth
- **Assistance**: Help in plant growth
- **Elegant Elongation**: Causing cells to elongate
- **Elevates**: The growth towards light direction.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]
**"Aunt's Elongating Plants Love Sunlight."**
:::

**Explanation:**  
In this mnemonic, we can remember the influence of auxins:

- **Aunt's**: Auxins
- **Elongating**: Cells elongate
- **Plants**: Indicative of plant growth
- **Love Sunlight**: In response to light direction.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]
**"Awesome Unicorns Lightly Glide, Cell Elongation Naturally Follows."**
:::

**Explanation:**  
A whimsical way to remember auxin's effect:

- **Awesome**: Auxins
- **Unicorns**: Help
- **Lightly Glide**: Growth by elongating
- **Cell Elongation**: Refers to elongation of cells
- **Naturally Follows**: Response to light.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]
**"Auxiliary Cells Ease into Light."**
:::

**Explanation:**  
A concise reminder of the role of auxins:

- **Auxiliary**: Auxins
- **Cells**: The cells affected
- **Ease into Light**: Represents elongation in response to light direction.
