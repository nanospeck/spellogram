# 6. Describe the primary functions of the human brain.

The brain, consisting of the fore-brain, mid-brain, and hind-brain, integrates sensory information, orchestrates motor responses, and controls complex behaviors. Use the mnemonics below to remember its primary functions.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**"For-sight Means Happy-brain: Senses, Moves & Behaves"**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps remember the key roles of different brain parts and their functions:

- **For-sight**: Fore-brain (integration of sensory information)
- **Means**: Mid-brain (orchestration of motor responses)
- **Happy-brain**: Hind-brain (control of complex behaviors)
- **Senses, Moves & Behaves**: Directly ties to the functions of integrating sensory info, orchestrating motor responses, and controlling behaviors.
