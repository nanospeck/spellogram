# 17. What feedback mechanism occurs if blood sugar levels rise significantly?

The pancreas detects high sugar levels and releases more insulin to lower it, exemplifying a feedback mechanism. Use the mnemonics below to understand its role in this process.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Panicked Insulin Detectives Lower Crime Efficiently**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps remember the key components:

- **Panicked**: The body's response to high sugar levels.
- **Insulin**: The hormone released by the pancreas.
- **Detectives**: The role of the pancreas in detecting sugar levels.
- **Lower**: Represents the action of lowering blood sugar.
- **Crime**: Symbolizes the high sugar levels.
- **Efficiently**: Reflects the effectiveness of the feedback mechanism.
