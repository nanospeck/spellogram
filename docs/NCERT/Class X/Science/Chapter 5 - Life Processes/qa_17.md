# 17. What is the role of guard cells in plants?

### Answer:
A: Guard cells regulate the opening and closing of stomata, controlling gas exchange and transpiration in plants.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]
**"Guardians of the Stomata: Keep Transpiration in Check."**

:::
**Mnemonic Explanation:**
This mnemonic positions "guard cells" as the "guardians" who manage the "stomata," emphasizing their crucial role in "controlling gas exchange and transpiration." It captures the responsibility of guard cells in regulating plant processes in a simple and memorable phrase.
