# 5. What are the requirements and products of photosynthesis?

A: Requirements: Sunlight, chlorophyll, carbon dioxide, and water. Products: Carbohydrates and oxygen.

Use the mnemonics below to understand and remember the requirements and products of photosynthesis.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Silly Cats Dance Wildly Creating Oxygen**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic uses a vibrant image of dancing cats to help recall the necessary components and results of photosynthesis:

- **Silly**: Sunlight
- **Cats**: Chlorophyll
- **Dance**: Carbon Dioxide
- **Wildly**: Water
- **Creating**: Carbohydrates
- **Oxygen**: Oxygen

---

- **Craves Oxygen Carbs**: Carbohydrates and Oxygen
