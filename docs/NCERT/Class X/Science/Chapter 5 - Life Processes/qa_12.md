# 12. What is the structure and function of alveoli?

Alveoli are tiny air sacs in the lungs with thin walls rich in blood vessels, designed to maximize the exchange of oxygen and carbon dioxide. Use the mnemonics below to understand their structure and function more clearly.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]
**Airy Thin Alveoli Exchange Oxygen Neatly**
:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps recall the essential features and function of alveoli:

- **Airy**: Alveoli are air sacs.
- **Thin**: They have thin walls.
- **Alveoli**: Refers to the alveoli.
- **Exchange**: Highlights their role in gas exchange.
- **Oxygen Neatly**: Emphasizes the efficient exchange of oxygen and carbon dioxide.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]
**Alveoli: Tiny-Air-Sacs With-Vessel-Walls, Maximize O2-CO2 Trades**
:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic breaks down the components of the alveoli:

- **Tiny-Air-Sacs**: To remember that alveoli are small air sacs.
- **With-Vessel-Walls**: To emphasize the walls rich in blood vessels.
- **Maximize O2-CO2 Trades**: To recall their function of maximizing oxygen and carbon dioxide exchange.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]
**Tiny Albert Volunteers To Maximize OxyCoco Exchange**
:::

**Explanation:**  
This creative mnemonic captures key elements:

- **Tiny**: Tiny air sacs.
- **Albert**: For alveoli.
- **Volunteers**: Walls rich in blood vessels.
- **To**: Thin walls.
- **Maximize**: High efficiency.
- **OxyCoco**: Oxygen and carbon dioxide.
- **Exchange**: Gas exchange process.
