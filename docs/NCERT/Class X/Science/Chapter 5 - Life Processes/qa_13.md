# 13. What is double circulation and why is it necessary?

Double circulation involves the separate circulation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood, which ensures efficient oxygen supply to organs and energy regulation in warm-blooded animals. Use the mnemonics below to understand its necessity.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Double Circles: Oxygen Dances, Energy Sings**

:::

**Explanation:**  
Each part of the mnemonic corresponds to a key part of the explanation of double circulation:

- **Double Circles**: Reminds you of the term "double circulation."
- **Oxygen Dances**: Helps recall the separate circulation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood.
- **Energy Sings**: Emphasizes the efficient oxygen supply and energy regulation in warm-blooded animals.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Double Deeds, Oxygen and Energy Neatly Supplied.**

:::

**Explanation:**  

- **Double**: Reminds you of "double circulation."
- **Deeds**: Stands for "circulation of deoxygenated blood."
- **Oxygen**: Represents the "circulation of oxygenated blood."
- **Energy**: Corresponds to the "energy regulation."
- **Neatly**: Suggests the efficiency of the system.
- **Supplied**: Implies the role of circulating blood in supplying oxygen.
