# 7. How do heterotrophs obtain nutrition?

Heterotrophs rely on complex substances derived from autotrophs or other organisms, which are broken down into simpler substances inside their bodies. Use the mnemonics below to understand how they obtain nutrition.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Heterotrophs Hunt Complex Dinner, Breaking Down Simply.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic helps recall parts of how heterotrophs obtain their nutrition:  

- **H**: Heterotrophs
- **Hunt**: Rely on obtaining substances (from other organisms)
- **Complex Dinner**: Complex substances from autotrophs or organisms
- **Breaking Down**: The process of breaking down these substances
- **Simply**: Into simpler substances

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Heteros Hitchhike for Homestyle Hamburgers, Dismantling Inside**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic offers a narrative to assist memory:  

- **Heteros**: Refers to "heterotrophs."
- **Hitchhike**: Implies reliance on others.
- **Homestyle Hamburgers**: Signifies complex substances akin to complete meals.
- **Dismantling Inside**: Represents breakdown into simpler substances inside their bodies.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Hungry Heterotrophs, Happily Healing by Humble Hunting**

:::

**Explanation:**  
The mnemonic breaks down the core elements of heterotrophic nutrition:  

- **Hungry Heterotrophs**: These organisms seek food from others.
- **Happily Healing**: Depend on complex substances essential for growth.
- **Humble Hunting**: Rely on autotrophs or other sources, breaking down complex compounds.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Hungry Elk Devour Apples, Crunch Otters Silently In Silence**

:::

**Explanation:**  
Key components of heterotroph nutrition are represented:  

- **Hungry**: Heterotrophs
- **Elk**: Rely on
- **Devour Apples**: Complex substances from autotrophs
- **Crunch Otters**: Or other organisms
- **Silently In Silence**: Broken down into simpler substances inside their bodies

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Complex Substance Hounds Derive Simple Snacks Internally.**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic emphasizes the process:  

- **Complex Substance**: The types of nutrition they rely on
- **Hounds**: Refers to heterotrophs
- **Derive**: Obtaining these from others
- **Simple Snacks**: Simplified forms
- **Internally**: Breakdown occurring within their bodies
