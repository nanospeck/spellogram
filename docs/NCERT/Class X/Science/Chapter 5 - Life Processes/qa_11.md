# 11. How is oxygen transported in human beings?

Oxygen is transported through the body bound to hemoglobin in red blood cells after being absorbed in the lungs from inhaled air. Utilize these mnemonics to help remember this process:

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Red Buses Have Oxygen Stops Across Lungs**

:::

**Explanation:** The mnemonic associates oxygen transport to a journey using red blood cells as red buses:
- **Red Buses**: Red blood cells, which carry oxygen.
- **Have**: Hemoglobin within these cells.
- **Oxygen Stops**: Oxygen binding to hemoglobin.
- **Across Lungs**: Where oxygen is absorbed before the journey begins.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Oxygen Bonds with Heroes in Red Chambers**

:::

**Explanation:** 
- **Oxygen**: The element being transported.
- **Bonds**: Oxygen attaches to hemoglobin.
- **Heroes**: Hemoglobin, doing the essential work.
- **Red Chambers**: Represents red blood cells, illustrating where the journey takes place.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Oxygen Travels Highways, Becoming Red-Carried Life**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **Oxygen**: The molecule being transported.
- **Travels**: Movement through the bloodstream.
- **Highways**: Blood vessels serve as pathways.
- **Becoming**: Action of binding with hemoglobin.
- **Red-Carried**: Carried by red blood cells as life’s vital oxygen.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**RBCs shuttle O2 gripping hemoglobin**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **RBCs**: Red Blood Cells, transporting units.
- **Shuttle**: Movement throughout the body.
- **O2**: Oxygen being transported.
- **Gripping**: Binding with hemoglobin, facilitating transportation.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Oxygen Hitches Hemoglobin's Ride In Red Balloons**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **Oxygen Hitches**: Oxygen binds to hemoglobin.
- **Hemoglobin's Ride**: The binding and transport mechanism.
- **In Red Balloons**: Red blood cells, symbolizing the transportation method post-absorption in the lungs.
