# 16. How do human kidneys function in excretion?

Human kidneys filter blood to remove wastes like urea, reabsorbing required substances and forming urine which is expelled via the urinary system. Use the mnemonics below to remember its roles.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Keri Filters Unnecessary Urine Excellently**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic encapsulates the primary functions of the kidneys:
- **Keri**: Represents **Kidneys**
- **Filters**: Indicates **filter blood**
- **Unnecessary**: Stands for **remove wastes like urea**
- **Urine**: Denotes **forming urine**
- **Excellently**: Symbolizes the final act of **expelling via the urinary system**

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**K-FUR (Kidneys Filter, Urea Removed)**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This concise mnemonic combines the essential aspects of kidney function:
- **K-FUR** simplifies the core process:
  - **K**: Kidneys
  - **F**: Filter blood
  - **U**: Urea is removed
  - **R**: Represents the subsequent reabsorption and formation

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Filtration Kings Remove Urea, Reabsorb, Urinate Efficiently**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic details each step of the kidney function:
- **Filtration Kings**: Highlight **kidneys filtering blood**
- **Remove Urea**: Emphasizes the removal of waste
- **Reabsorb**: Indicates reabsorbing essential substances
- **Urinate Efficiently**: Refers to forming and expelling urine

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Keen Filtration, Urea Exit, Required Substance Reshuffle, Urine Ushers Out**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic systematically covers the excretion process:
- **Keen Filtration**: Denotes filtering of blood
- **Urea Exit**: Indicates removal of waste like urea
- **Required Substance Reshuffle**: Refers to reabsorbing necessary substances
- **Urine Ushers Out**: Represents urine formation and expulsion

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Filtration, Reabsorption, Formation - Urine's Final Station**

:::

**Explanation:**  
This mnemonic aligns with the sequential process undertaken by kidneys:
- **Filtration**: Starts with **filtering blood**
- **Reabsorption**: Continues with **reabsorbing substances**
- **Formation**: Proceeds to **forming urine**
- **Urine's Final Station**: Culminates in **expelling via the urinary system**
