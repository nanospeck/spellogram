# 15. How do plants excrete waste products?

### Answer:
A: Plants excrete waste through processes such as transpiration, passage into soil, or storage in leaves and old xylem.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Plants Transpire Soil Slowly**

:::

**Mnemonic Explanation:**

- **Plants**: Refers to the subject, plants.
- **Transpire**: Connects to the process of transpiration.
- **Soil**: Implies the passage of waste into soil.
- **Slowly**: Reminds that waste is stored in leaves and old xylem, hinting at storage that does not require rapid expulsion.

Each word captures the key ways plants manage their waste products.
