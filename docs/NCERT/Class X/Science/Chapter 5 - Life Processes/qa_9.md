# 9. How is food transported in the human body?

Food is transported from the digestive system to body cells via the bloodstream, where digested nutrients are absorbed and circulated. Here are some mnemonics to help you remember the transport process:

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Digestive Bull Ships Nutrients Onboard**

:::

**Explanation:** This mnemonic guides you through the transportation process:

- **Digestive**: Starts from the digestive system
- **Bull**: Bloodstream symbolizes the bull (or the main channel)
- **Ships**: Represents the transportation process
- **Nutrients**: Refers to the digested nutrients
- **Onboard**: Circulation throughout the body, like nutrients being 'onboard' for delivery
---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Fast Trains Deliver Calories to Body Stations**

:::

**Explanation:** Each part of this phrase represents different steps of the transport of food:

- **Fast**: Food
- **Trains**: Transported
- **Deliver**: Digestive system
- **Calories**: Circulated nutrients
- **Body**: Body cells
- **Stations**: Stream via bloodstream
---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Food Travels By Digestive Sea - Absorbed, Circulated Sweetly**

:::

**Explanation:** This mnemonic tells a story:

- **Digestive Sea**: Imagines food on a journey starting from the digestive system
- **Absorbed**: Refers to the digested nutrients
- **Circulated Sweetly**: Describes the circulation via bloodstream
---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Food Travels Swiftly, Digesting Nutrients Boldly**

:::

**Explanation:** This mnemonic captures the essence of food transport:

- **Food**: Refers to the starting material, food itself
- **Travels**: Implies transportation through the body
- **Swiftly**: Movement via bloodstream
- **Digesting**: Highlights the role of digested nutrients
- **Nutrients**: Emphasizes the role of nutrients
- **Boldly**: Suggests complete distribution and absorption in the body
---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Friendly Ships Voyage Deeply, Carrying Nutritional Absorbents**

:::

**Explanation:** This mnemonic helps connect each part of the transport process:

- **Friendly**: Food
- **Ships**: Represents the system, here the digestive system
- **Voyage**: Signifies the pathway through which nutrients travel, via the bloodstream
- **Deeply**: Refers to the digested nutrients
- **Carrying**: Implies circulation throughout the body
- **Nutritional Absorbents**: Conveys absorption and assimilation of nutrients into cells
