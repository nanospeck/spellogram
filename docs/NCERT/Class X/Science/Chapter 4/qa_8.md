# 8. What happens during the combustion of hydrocarbons?

Hydrocarbons burn in oxygen to produce carbon dioxide, water, heat, and light. Use the mnemonics below to remember the process.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Hot Candles Will Heat Lovingly**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **H**ot: Hydrocarbons
- **C**andles: Carbon dioxide
- **W**ill: Water
- **H**eat: Heat
- **L**ovingly: Light

This mnemonic captures the key products of hydrocarbon combustion, using vivid imagery of a candle for the process.


:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Hot Dogs Can't Wait Half Longingly**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **H**ot: Heat
- **D**ogs: Dioxide (Carbon Dioxide)
- **C**an't: Carbon
- **W**ait: Water
- **H**alf: Heat
- **L**ongingly: Light

Helpful in associating the sequence with everyday objects like a hot dog stand.


:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Hot H2O Light for CO2 Production**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **Hot**: Heat Production
- **H2O**: Water
- **Light**: Emission of light
- **CO2 Production**: Carbon dioxide generation

A succinct phrase summarizing combustion outcomes.


:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Hearty Camps With Cool Light**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **Hearty**: Heat
- **Camps**: Carbon dioxide
- **With**: Water
- **Cool**: Carbon dioxide
- **Light**: Light

This mnemonic evokes a camping scene, making the reaction's elements memorable through a relatable scenario.


:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Hot Dogs Will Light Candles**

:::

**Explanation:**
- **Hot**: Heat
- **Dogs**: Carbon dioxide
- **Will**: Water
- **Light**: Light
- **Candles**: Represents burning/oxidation process

An illustrative phrase providing quick recall for hydrocarbon combustion and its products.
