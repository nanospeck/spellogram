# 11. How does ethanoic acid react with sodium bicarbonate? What are the products?

Ethanoic acid reacts with sodium bicarbonate to produce sodium acetate, carbon dioxide, and water (CH3COOH + NaHCO3 → CH3COONa + H2O + CO2).

Use the mnemonics below to understand this reaction.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Every Soda's New Carbonated Sparkling Water.**

:::

**Explanation:**

This mnemonic simplifies memorizing each component in the reaction and their products:

- **E**: Ethanoic acid (CH3COOH)
- **S**: Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3)
- **N**: New (→)
- **C**: Carbonated (CO2)
- **S**: Sparkling (Sodium acetate, CH3COONa)
- **W**: Water (H2O)


---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**Every Senior Ate Cakes, Cooking Ovens**

:::

**Explanation:**

With a focus on the steps of the reaction:

- **E**: Ethanoic acid
- **S**: Sodium bicarbonate
- **A**: Acetate
- **C**: Carbon dioxide
- **C**: Cooking (Carbon dioxide)
- **O**: Ovens (Water)
