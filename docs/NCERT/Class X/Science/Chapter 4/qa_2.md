# 2. Why can't carbon easily form C4⁻ anions or C4⁺ cations?

Forming C4⁻ anions would require carbon to gain 4 electrons, making it difficult for the nucleus with 6 protons to hold 10 electrons. Loss of 4 electrons to form C4⁺ cations would require a significant amount of energy. Use the mnemonics below to understand why.

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**Carbon 'C' Struggles To Gain or Lose 4, Like Adding a Ton or Jumping a Floor.**

:::

- **Explanation:**  
  This mnemonic helps to remember the difficulties for carbon in gaining or losing 4 electrons:

  - **"Carbon 'C'"**: It's about carbon.
  - **"Struggles"**: Difficulty in gaining/losing electrons.
  - **"To Gain or Lose 4"**: Gaining 4 electrons (C4⁻) or losing 4 electrons (C4⁺).
  - **"Like Adding a Ton"**: Difficult for 6 protons to hold 10 electrons.
  - **"Jumping a Floor"**: Significant energy required for electron loss.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 2]

**C4 Trouble: Gain or Drain? Protons Strain, Energy Pain.**

:::

- **Explanation:**  
  This mnemonic emphasizes the struggle carbon faces:

  - **"C4 Trouble"**: Carbon's challenge with C4⁻ and C4⁺.
  - **"Gain"**: Difficulty in gaining 4 electrons for anions.
  - **"Drain"**: Energy needed to lose 4 electrons for cations.
  - **"Protons Strain"**: Struggle for the nucleus to maintain balance with 10 electrons.
  - **"Energy Pain"**: High energy required for electron loss.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 3]

**Gain 4, Lose 4 - Carbon's Core Can't Hold or Afford.**

:::

- **Explanation:**  
  This mnemonic outlines the core difficulties:

  - **"Gain 4"**: Requiring to gain 4 electrons for C4⁻ anions.
  - **"Lose 4"**: Needing to lose 4 electrons for C4⁺ cations.
  - **"Carbon's Core"**: Carbon's 6-proton nucleus.
  - **"Can't Hold or Afford"**: Difficulty holding extra electrons and energy cost of losing electrons.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 4]

**Four Electrons? Carbon Stalls: Gains Make Ten, Loss Met With Energy Drain.**

:::

- **Explanation:**  
  This mnemonic explains carbon’s halt:

  - **"Four Electrons?"**: Gaining/losing 4 electrons.
  - **"Carbon Stalls"**: Difficulty in forming C4⁻ or C4⁺.
  - **"Gains Make Ten"**: Difficulty managing 10 electrons.
  - **"Loss Met With Energy Drain"**: Energy needed to lose 4 electrons.

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 5]

**Carbon's 4-Corner Dilemma: Gaining is Too Heavy, Losing is Too Spendy.**

:::

- **Explanation:**  
  Highlights the dilemma for carbon:

  - **"Carbon's 4-Corner Dilemma"**: Difficulty in forming either anions or cations.
  - **"Gaining is Too Heavy"**: Difficulty of 6-proton nucleus managing 10 electrons.
  - **"Losing is Too Spendy"**: Significant energy needed for electron loss.
