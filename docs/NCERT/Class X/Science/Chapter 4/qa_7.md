# 7. What is a homologous series in organic chemistry? Give an example.

### Answer:
A homologous series is a series of compounds with the same functional group and similar chemical properties, differing by a CH2 unit. Example: Alkanes (methane, ethane, propane).

Use the mnemonics below to understand the concept of a homologous series in organic chemistry:

---

:::tip[Mnemonic 1]

**"Funny Bunny's CH2 Parade, Methane to Propane!"**

:::

**Explanation:**  
Visualize a "Funny Bunny" leading a parade in which each row showcases a group of compounds differentiated by a CH2 unit. The series comprises methane, ethane, and propane, illustrating structured similarity.
